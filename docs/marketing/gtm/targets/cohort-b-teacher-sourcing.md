# Cohort B (Proof Cohort) — Teacher Sourcing Playbook

**Goal:** Recruit a **proof cohort of 4–6 grades 9–12 ELA teachers** who will grade real student essays across **≥4 assignment batches** under a **signed school data-protection agreement (DPA)**, for a pre-registered academic-style study proving aiTA converges on each teacher's feedback voice. Student names de-identified before processing. Incentive: free full access + product-shaping input.

**Key constraint relaxation:** Warm / related-party contacts are FINE for this PROOF cohort. Only the *revenue* cohort must be strangers. That means your own network (former teachers, alumni networks, friends-of-friends in education) is the fastest channel and should run in parallel with the cold channels below.

> **Sourcing rigor note:** Every channel below has a real, working URL verified via web search (June 2026). Member counts and "recruitment allowed?" judgments are marked **[Unverified]** where they come from secondary listicles or general platform norms rather than a direct read of the group's current pinned rules. Confirm the rule before posting in any moderated group.

## 2. DPA / district-approval reality — how hard is the "signed DPA" ask?

**Bottom line: a teacher usually CANNOT sign a binding student-data DPA solo. The DPA is an institutional contract (district ↔ vendor), so the signer is almost always a district administrator, IT/data-privacy officer, or legal — not the classroom teacher.** Plan the recruitment around the teacher being your *champion who walks the agreement through their district*, not the signatory.

### The frameworks you'll meet

| Framework | What it is | Who signs | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SDPC NDPA** (National Data Privacy Agreement, v2 — Apr 2024) | The de-facto standard K-12 DPA. 130k+ signed DPAs on the SDPC registry; 28 state alliances. Used when a vendor processes student PII as a "school official." | District ↔ vendor (institutional). | https://privacy.a4l.org/national-dpa/ |
| **SDPC National *Research* DPA (NRDPA)** | A standardized agreement specifically for sharing student data with **researchers** under FERPA's "studies/research exception." **This is the most relevant template for aiTA's pre-registered study.** | School/district ↔ researcher (institutional). | https://privacy.a4l.org/national-research-dpa/ |
| **State-specific DPAs / exhibits** | Many states add a state addendum to the NDPA (e.g., CA, NY, IL student-privacy laws). | District ↔ vendor + state exhibit. | https://privacy.a4l.org/resources/ |

### How the FERPA pathways change the ask (this is the lever)

1. **De-identified data → lightest path.** FERPA permits use of **properly de-identified** student data without the consent/DPA machinery that identifiable records require. aiTA already de-identifies student names before processing. *If* the essays reaching aiTA carry no PII (names stripped, no other identifiers, school can't be reverse-mapped), the strict DPA requirement softens substantially. Sources: Public Interest Privacy Center (https://publicinterestprivacy.org/edtech-data-sharing/), iKeepSafe FERPA 101 (https://ikeepsafe.org/content/uploads/2017/01/FERPA-101-for-EdTech-iKeepSafe.pdf), ED Vendor FAQ (https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/sites/default/files/resource_document/file/Vendor%20FAQ.pdf).
2. **"School official" exception** — if a teacher uses aiTA with real (even pseudonymized but re-identifiable) student work, the school is likely disclosing education records to a vendor, which triggers the DPA. A teacher signing up for a free tier does **not** waive this — the obligation sits with the school. Source: Public Interest Privacy Center (above).
3. **FERPA "studies" exception** — for a formal research study, the NRDPA is the written agreement FERPA requires. It is signed by the district, not the teacher.

### What this means operationally

- **Ask the teacher to be the internal sponsor**, then route the DPA to whoever owns student-data-privacy at their district (titles vary: "Director of Technology", "Data Privacy Officer", "Assistant Superintendent"). Screen for whether the teacher has a working relationship with that person.
- **Lead with de-identification** in every conversation — it is your single biggest friction-reducer. Have a one-page "Data Handling & De-identification Summary" ready to hand to the district privacy officer.
- **Pre-fill the SDPC NDPA / NRDPA** so the district reviews a familiar template, not a bespoke contract. Districts approve SDPC-format agreements far faster than novel ones (that's the whole point of the consortium).
- **Reality check on difficulty:** Solo teacher consent is realistic ONLY for a fully de-identified pilot framed as non-PII. The moment identifiable student records are involved, expect a 2–8 week district review. Budget for this in the sprint (Section 5) by recruiting a slightly larger funnel and prioritizing teachers who already have district admin buy-in or who teach where the SDPC NDPA is already adopted (check the SDPC registry for their district).

## 3. Incentive norms — what teachers expect to participate

Documented norms from edtech pilots and research studies:

| Incentive | Typical form | Evidence / source |
|---|---|---|
| **Free full access to the tool** | Comp'd license for pilot + often beyond. Standard baseline; often sufficient on its own for product-shaping pilots. | Learning Accelerator "Empowering Teachers to Pilot Edtech" (https://practices.learningaccelerator.org/strategies/empowering-teachers-to-pilot-edtech-tools); Digital Promise EdTech Pilot Framework (https://digitalpromise.org/edtech-pilot-framework/). |
| **Stipend / honorarium** | Real money for real time. Edtech PD/pilot stipends documented at **$750/school year**; paid research panels pay **$4–$5 per short survey** and **$13–20/hr** for educator time. A multi-week grading study warrants a meaningful honorarium (a few hundred $ range is normal for this commitment). | Teacher Innovation Network stipend FAQ (https://sites.google.com/scoe.net/innovativeeducator/california-educator-stipend-faq); The 74 substitute stipend study (https://www.the74million.org/article/new-study-40-stipend-draws-substitute-teachers-into-hard-to-staff-schools/); Prolific educator-pay norms (https://www.prolific.com/participants). |
| **PD / continuing-education credit** | Letter or hours teachers can apply to license renewal. Highly valued, low cost to you. | Common edtech-PD norm; Digital Promise framework emphasizes professional-learning structure (above). Confirm whether the teacher's district/state accepts an external PD letter. **[Unverified per state]** |
| **Co-authorship / named acknowledgment** | For a pre-registered academic-style study, offering acknowledgment (or co-authorship for substantive contributors) is a genuine, career-relevant draw and standard research practice. | Research-ethics authorship norms (PSU BIOET 533: https://courses.ems.psu.edu/bioet533/node/7); AI-in-education studies routinely acknowledge participating teachers (arXiv teacher-AI collab papers). |
| **Product-shaping voice** | Direct line to the team; feature input; "design partner" status. | Standard pilot motivator (Learning Accelerator, above). |

**Recommended stack for aiTA's proof cohort:** Free full access (baseline) + a modest honorarium per completed batch (ties payment to the ≥4-batch commitment and reduces dropout) + named acknowledgment in the pre-registered study + optional PD letter + design-partner status. Tying part of the incentive to *batch completion* is the key retention lever for a 6-week, 4-batch commitment.

## 4. Screening checklist (qualify before you spend a DPA cycle on them)

Use as a short form (Google Form / Typeform) gated on the channels above. Must clear **all** of these:

- [ ] **Role:** Currently teaches **grades 9–12 ELA** (English/Language Arts), in a US school. *(AP Lang/Lit is a strong-fit bonus.)*
- [ ] **Grades with rubrics:** Regularly grades **real student essays against a rubric** (not just multiple-choice / auto-graded work).
- [ ] **Volume:** Has **≥ ~25 students per essay batch** (enough signal per batch for the convergence analysis).
- [ ] **Commitment:** Willing to grade **≥ 4 assignment batches over ~6 weeks** (set the cadence expectation up front — this is where dropout happens).
- [ ] **DPA reachability:** Can identify the person at their school/district who handles student-data-privacy / DPAs, AND believes they can get a **de-identified-pilot DPA signed** (or their district already has an SDPC NDPA on file — check the registry).
- [ ] **De-identification OK:** Comfortable that student names are stripped before processing and willing to follow the de-identification workflow.
- [ ] **Study consent:** Willing to participate in a **pre-registered study** (some logging/comparison of their feedback) and be named/acknowledged.
- [ ] **Tech baseline:** Can upload essays / use a web app; reliable email responsiveness.

**Scoring:** Hard-pass on any of role / rubric-grading / 4-batch commitment / DPA reachability. The DPA item is the highest-risk gate — weight it heavily and over-recruit accordingly.

## 5. Two-week recruitment sprint (15–20 candidate funnel → land 4–6)

**Funnel math:** Expect heavy attrition at the DPA gate. To land 4–6 signed, run **15–20 screened-qualified** candidates, which means reaching **several hundred** teachers across channels. Front-load warm/related-party contacts (allowed for the proof cohort) — they convert fastest and several can bypass the cold-channel friction entirely.

### Pre-work (Day 0, before sprint)
- Write the **screener form** (Section 4) and a **one-paragraph study call** (what, time commitment, incentives, de-identification, DPA-as-sponsor framing).
- Prepare the **DPA packet**: pre-filled SDPC NDPA + NRDPA, plus a one-page **Data Handling & De-identification Summary** for district privacy officers.
- Draft mod-outreach DM templates for FB groups + Reddit (lead with ethics/privacy + IRB-style framing).

### Week 1 — Cast wide + warm
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fire **all warm/related-party contacts** (former-teacher network, alumni, friends-of-friends in 9–12 ELA). Post screener to **r/SampleSize** (no mod gate). |
| 1–2 | Message mods of **r/ELATeachers, r/Teachers**, and the **AP Lang / AP Lit / 2ndary ELA / High School ELA** Facebook groups asking permission + sharing the privacy summary. Don't post until cleared. |
| 2–3 | Email **5–10 NCTE state affiliates** (start CATE, TCTELA, NYSEC + your home state) + NCTE research-committee pathway, requesting a newsletter/listserv blurb. Email **2–3 university English-ed field-placement offices** for cooperating-teacher referrals. |
| 3–4 | Engage on **Bluesky ELA starter packs + #aplitchat / #engchat**: follow, reply, then warm-DM strong-fit teachers. |
| 4–5 | (Optional, budget-permitting) Launch a **Prolific** teacher-filtered screener to seed the funnel fast. |
| 5–7 | Post in any **approved** FB/Reddit channels. Triage incoming screener responses daily; book 15-min calls with qualified leads. |

### Week 2 — Close + DPA
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 8–9 | Run 15-min qualifying calls. Confirm the 4-batch commitment and the DPA path. Hand the DPA packet to teachers whose districts need it; for already-de-identified/SDPC-adopted districts, fast-track. |
| 9–11 | Push DPA signatures: connect with each teacher's district privacy officer; offer to join a call to answer data questions. Send the pre-filled SDPC template. |
| 11–12 | Lock incentives in writing (access + per-batch honorarium + acknowledgment + PD letter). Send onboarding + batch-1 instructions. |
| 12–13 | Backfill: if <6 confirmed, re-hit warm contacts and any still-open approved channels; second-touch unresponsive qualified leads. |
| 14 | **Target state: 4–6 teachers signed (DPA in motion or done), batch-1 scheduled.** Confirm batch cadence so all complete ≥4 within the 6-week study window. |

**Risk controls:** (1) The DPA gate is the bottleneck — prioritize teachers with existing district buy-in or SDPC-adopted districts; (2) tie part of the honorarium to batch completion to fight 6-week dropout; (3) over-recruit by 50% (aim ~8 confirmed to safely net 4–6 that finish).
