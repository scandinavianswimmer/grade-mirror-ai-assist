# aiTA GTM Target List — Facebook Groups, Newsletters, Influencers & Communities

**Goal:** acquire arms-length stranger HS ELA/humanities teachers (NOT founder's network) ahead of the Build with Gemini XPRIZE launch (deadline Aug 17, 2026).

**Strategy guardrails baked into every "how aiTA gets in" note below:**
- Facebook groups = #1 channel, but **direct self-promo = permanent ban.** Play value-first, proxy through teacher-users, and free-PD webinars.
- "Borrowed channels" = teacher influencers / newsletters / podcasts whose audience overlaps our ICP.

**Honesty flags:** Member counts and self-promo rules for most FB groups are NOT published on the open web — Facebook gates group "About/Rules" behind login, so I marked them **(unverified)** and noted the *category default* (almost all teacher groups ban unsolicited vendor self-promo; many run a designated weekly "promo/self-promo thread" or "Tuesday/Friday share day"). To confirm exact rules and counts, you (or a teacher-user proxy) must open each group while logged in and read the Rules tab. I flagged where data was genuinely thin.

1. **2ndary ELA Facebook group** — largest, most concentrated HS-ELA audience that exists, and it's our exact ICP. Highest organic-acquisition ceiling. Motion: value-first contribution + teacher-user proxies + pitch admins a free PD webinar. (Confirm count/rules logged-in first.)
