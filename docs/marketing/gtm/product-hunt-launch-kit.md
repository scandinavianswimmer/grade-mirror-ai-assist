## Launch-day comment bank (maker replies)
- **"How is this different from EssayGrader/CoGrader?"** → "Those give you more rubrics or a 'sounds like
  you' prompt setting. aiTA runs a learning loop that measurably converges on *your* voice — and we
  pre-registered a study to prove it. It also refuses off-topic work instead of rubber-stamping it."
- **"Does it replace teachers?"** → "Opposite — you're the final grader by design. It's a co-pilot you
  supervise; auto-finalize only publishes the high-confidence, on-topic ones, and routes anything tricky
  to you."
- **"What about student privacy / FERPA?"** → "Student names are stripped before anything reaches the
  model, data is owner-scoped and erasable, and the trial runs on sample essays — no student data at all.
  We never claim 'fully compliant'; we tell you exactly what we do."
- **"Accuracy?"** → "It grades to your rubric with per-criterion evidence, recomputes totals
  server-side, and withholds when it's not confident or the work is off-topic. You approve everything."
- **"Pricing?"** → "$15/mo or ~$150/yr, 14-day full trial, no card. There's a free 15-grade/month tier too."
