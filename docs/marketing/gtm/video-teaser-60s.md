## Master script (16:9 — PH gallery / landing / YouTube)

Format: `[TIME] ON-SCREEN — TEXT OVERLAY — VO`

```
[0:00–0:04]  ON-SCREEN: a tall stack of essays; a clock ticking past midnight.
TEXT: "150 essays. Again."
VO: "Every English teacher knows this stack."

[0:04–0:10]  ON-SCREEN: a generic, robotic AI comment fades in, then gets an eye-roll cursor.
TEXT: "AI grading? Robotic. Generic. You redo it anyway."
VO: "And they know why AI grading never stuck — it doesn't sound like them."

[0:10–0:26]  ON-SCREEN: click "Grade all" on a 25-essay batch. The named agents animate; grades populate
            on their own. Counter lands on "22 auto-finalized · 3 routed to you."
TEXT: "aiTA grades the set. Unattended."
VO: "aiTA grades the whole set for you — and only sends the tricky three to your desk."

[0:26–0:40]  ON-SCREEN: the voice toggle flips — "generic draft" → "in your voice" — visibly different.
TEXT: "In YOUR voice. It learns you."
VO: "The feedback reads like you wrote it — because it learns your voice from every edit."

[0:40–0:50]  ON-SCREEN: an off-topic essay shows a WITHHELD grade + reason. A green "Approve" tap.
TEXT: "Refuses off-topic work. You stay the final grader."
VO: "It refuses to grade what it shouldn't — and you approve everything."

[0:50–0:60]  ON-SCREEN: logo + URL.
TEXT: "aiTA — your voice. You stay the teacher.  ·  Free 14-day trial, no card."
VO: "aiTA. The grading runs itself. You stay in command."
```

## Hook A/B (first 3 seconds — test these)
- **A (relief):** "150 essays. Again." *(default)*
- **B (the magic):** open cold on the batch auto-grading — TEXT: "Watch AI grade 25 essays. Unattended."
- **C (voice):** the voice toggle flip — TEXT: "Same essay. Generic AI vs. YOUR voice."

## Vertical cut (9:16 — IG Reels / TikTok / Shorts / FB)
Same beats, retimed tighter (people drop fast on vertical):
- 0:00–0:03 Hook B or C (lead with the magic, not the problem — vertical needs an instant payoff).
- 0:03–0:18 Unattended batch grading + the "22 auto-finalized / 3 routed" counter.
- 0:18–0:30 Voice toggle.
- 0:30–0:40 Off-topic refusal + approve.
- 0:40–0:50 CTA end card.
Keep captions large, center-safe (avoid the bottom UI overlay zone). Trending-audio-friendly (works muted).
