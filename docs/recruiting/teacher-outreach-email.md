# Proof-cohort recruiting — copy-paste outreach

Goal: recruit **4–6 grades 9–12 ELA teachers** to grade real essays across ≥4 batches under a signed
school DPA, for the pre-registered voice-convergence proof (Cohort B). This is the longest-lead
Week-1 item — send it first. Target teachers you can reach warmly (related-party for *proof* is fine;
related-party only disqualifies *revenue*).

---

## Email A — warm intro (a teacher you know / a referral)

**Subject:** Quick favor — would you test an AI grader that learns *your* voice?

Hi {First name},

I'm building **aiTA**, an essay-grading assistant for ELA teachers. Unlike the others, it's designed
to learn *your* feedback voice over a few assignment sets — so the comments read like you wrote them,
not like a generic bot. I'm running a small, rigorous study to prove it actually works (or doesn't),
and I'd love you in it.

What it involves, over ~4–6 weeks:
- Grade **4 sets** of your real student essays in aiTA (you'd grade them anyway — this just drafts
  the feedback for you to approve or tweak).
- A one-tap "how much did you change it?" after each set. That's the whole burden.

In return: **free full access** for the study and after, and you shape a tool built for ELA teachers.
Your district signs a short data-protection agreement first (I'll send it), and student names are
stripped before anything is processed.

Want in? Reply and I'll send the agreement + a 10-minute setup link.

Thanks,
{Your name}
